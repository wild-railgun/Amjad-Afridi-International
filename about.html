Biography Sample

Stillness, control and an eye for light.

Amjad Afridi is a fashion and commercial model whose work moves between low-key studio editorials, outdoor campaign imagery and street-style shoots. His look suits tailoring and leather as naturally as a simple white tee.

On set he is calm, prepared and easy to direct — comfortable holding a still frame for a fragrance close-up or working with movement for a full-length look.

Approach Sample

Every frame is a collaboration.

Amjad treats each shoot as a conversation with the photographer: reading the light, finding the angle that serves the clothes and holding it.

His portfolio spans dramatic dark-studio portraits, natural-light park editorials and a sunlit fragrance campaign shot outdoors against modern architecture.

He is available for editorial, campaign, e-commerce, runway and brand-partnership work, and travels for bookings.
